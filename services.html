Services

Practical support for complex projects, programs and portfolios.

From project leadership and financial control to recovery, process improvement and change, I help organizations bring structure, visibility and control to complex work.

How I can help

Services

Choose one area of support or combine services based on what your project, program or team needs.

01 / Project & Program Management

Need someone to lead the work?

Hands-on leadership for complex projects and programs.

  • End-to-end project or program management
  • Project kickoff and delivery leadership
  • Scope, timelines, milestones and ownership
  • Risk, issue and dependency management
  • Cross-functional team leadership
  • Vendor, contractor and stakeholder coordination
  • Governance and status reporting
02 / Project Financial & Cost Control

Need better control of the numbers?

Cost and financial control that connects actual spend, commitments and remaining work to the forecast.

  • Budget development & control
  • Cost-to-complete / Estimate at Completion forecasting
  • Actuals, commitments & accrual reconciliation
  • Budget vs. actual vs. forecast variance analysis
  • Remaining-cost and funding forecasts
  • Change and cost-impact visibility
  • Monthly financial & executive reporting
03 / Program Controls & Portfolio Oversight

Have many projects but no single view?

Bring separate initiatives together so they can be managed as one program or portfolio.

  • Program and portfolio structure
  • Consolidated project dashboards
  • Cross-project timelines and dependencies
  • Portfolio financials and forecasting
  • Resource and priority visibility
  • Risk and issue consolidation
  • Executive and Board-ready reporting
04 / Project Setup & Planning

Need to turn an idea into an executable plan?

Create the structure needed before delivery begins.

  • SOW development
  • Scope and requirements definition
  • Estimates and budgets
  • Timeline and milestone creation
  • Work breakdown and ownership
  • Project kickoff
  • Governance and reporting setup
05 / Project Recovery & Stabilization

Is the project already off track?

Establish the truth, determine what remains and build a credible path forward.

  • Current-state assessment
  • Financial and delivery reconciliation
  • Remaining-scope assessment
  • Recovery and completion planning
  • Revised cost and schedule forecast
  • Risk, dependency and priority review
  • Executive recovery reporting
06 / PM Training & Delivery Improvement

Want your team to manage projects better?

Practical training and tools based on how project teams actually work.

  • Project management training and coaching
  • Financial management for project managers
  • Forecasting and project controls practices
  • SOW, estimation and planning practices
  • Templates, tools and reporting standards
  • PM processes and ways of working
  • Governance and operating-rhythm design
07 / Process Improvement

Need a better way of working?

Understand what is happening today, identify what is getting in the way and design a more effective process.

  • Current-state process mapping
  • Stakeholder interviews & discovery
  • Pain-point and root-cause analysis
  • Future-state process design
  • Roles, workflows & handoffs
  • Process implementation
  • Documentation, tools & templates
08 / Change Management

Need people to adopt a new process, system or way of working?

Plan and support the people side of change so new ways of working are understood, adopted and sustained.

  • Change impact assessment
  • Stakeholder analysis & engagement
  • Change and adoption planning
  • Communications planning
  • Training & enablement
  • Implementation support
  • Adoption & reinforcement
